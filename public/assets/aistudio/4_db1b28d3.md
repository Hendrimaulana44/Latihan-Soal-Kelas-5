## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a focused, reassuring, and productive EdTech environment tailored for primary school educators and young students at SDN 1 Purwasari. The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate Clarity** with **Playful EdTech Approachability**, prioritizing low cognitive load for teachers building test questions and high legibility and encouragement for elementary students taking digital assessments.

### Personality & Values
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Clean structures and deep royal blues evoke institutional reliability, accuracy, and official academic standards.
- **Supportive & Encouraging:** Warm amber-orange accents and gentle emerald positive feedback reduce test anxiety and make repetitive grading intuitive.
- **Focused & Uncluttered:** Ample whitespace, generous line heights, and soft rounded contours ensure reading comprehension remains effortless on both mobile devices and desktop screens.

### Target Audience & Dynamics
1. **Primary School Teachers (SD):** Need high-density utility, predictable form layouts, clear generation statuses, and instant export/print previews without UI clutter.
2. **Elementary Students (Grades 1–6):** Require large touch targets, unmistakable selected states, calm question pacing, and encouraging status indicators.
3. **School Administrator:** Needs macro-level oversight, clean tabular data summaries, and reliable theme controls.

## Colors

The palette employs a grounded, authoritative navy foundation paired with an active, approachable warm accent. Light mode serves as the canonical environment, reflecting traditional assessment paper clarity while utilizing modern display ergonomics.

### Hierarchy & Role Allocation
- **Primary Navy (`#1E3A8A` / `#2563EB`):** Applied to primary navigation headers, major titles, brand identifiers, progress rails, and default primary interactive states.
- **Secondary Orange (`#EA580C` / `#F97316`):** Reserved for high-value calls-to-action (e.g., "Generate Naskah", "Mulai Ujian", "Kirim Jawaban") and pending examination status tags.
- **Success Emerald (`#059669`):** Indicates verified test keys, passing student scores, completed question generation steps, and active server sync states.
- **Neutral Surface & Slate (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#E2E8F0`):** Provides a clean, paper-inspired backdrop that minimizes eye fatigue during extended editing sessions and digital exam taking.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic applications. Its open aperture, contemporary geometric geometry, and clean legibility cater to both early-grade reading comprehension and professional editorial workflows.

- **Exam Stimulus & Reading Passages:** Formatted in `body-lg` (18px) with relaxed leading (`28px`) to ensure younger elementary students maintain focus and tracking across long paragraphs.
- **Numbers & Question Indexing:** Employs medium to bold weights (`600` / `700`) to guarantee unmistakable identification when students toggle between question sheets and exam grids.
- **Print Mode Overrides:** In print preview sheets (A4 document view), typography condenses to standard print metrics (`11pt` body with `14pt` leading) to adhere to national standardized test layout expectations.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced fluid-grid structure supports both responsive web application behavior (desktop dashboards and mobile test forms) and strict proportional layouts (A4 document preview).

### Grid Foundation
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout with `2rem` outer padding and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum application canvas caps at `1280px` for optimal scanning.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` safe margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Essential for student testing setups on handheld phones.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- **Question Stems & Options:** Formatted with `space-md` (16px) vertical stack spacing, preventing mis-taps on small devices.
- **Container Padding:** Standard cards use `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- **Toolbar & Action Clusters:** Utilize `space-sm` (12px) horizontal gaps between related controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle atmospheric layering and soft, low-contrast shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphism or stark brutalist borders. This reflects a clean modern web application that prints cleanly and renders quickly inside Google Apps Script wrappers.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main workspace background (`#F8FAFC`). Uses a subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) for structural divisions.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Applied to question containers, syllabus cards, and summary stats. Uses a single diffuse drop-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Navigation Bar):** Applied to active cards, sticky exam header navigation, and dropdown menus: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Floating Trays):** Applied to question editor dialogs, exam submission alerts, and AI generation status sheets: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 4 (Focus Rings):** Interactive accessibility outline using a high-contrast offset: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system establishes a welcoming, child-friendly, yet disciplined geometry based on `Level 2` roundedness:
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Standardized to `rounded-lg` (8px to 10px) to balance friendly softness with reliable form density.
- **Cards & Stimulus Containers:** Styled with `rounded-xl` (16px) corners, providing distinctive modularity between separate exam questions and syllabus inputs.
- **Question Number Badges & Progress Dots:** Styled as full pill or circle contours (`9999px`) for quick scanning.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Generate / Submit):** Solid vibrant orange (`#EA580C`) background with white bold text, subtle shadow, and 10px corner radius. Minimum touch height of 48px on mobile for easy finger targeting.
- **Secondary Action (Edit / Save Draft):** Solid royal blue (`#1E3A8A` or `#2563EB`) background with white text, providing clear authority.
- **Outline / Ghost (Cancel / View Preview):** White surface with 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1` and navy text. Subtle slate background on hover (`#F1F5F9`).

### 2. Badges & Chips
- **Subject Indicators:** Matematika labeled with Royal Blue tint (`bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 border-blue-200`); Bahasa Indonesia labeled with Amber tint (`bg-amber-50 text-amber-800 border-amber-200`).
- **Exam Status Chips:** "Draft" (Slate outline), "Final" (Emerald filled badge with white icon), "Sedang Dikerjakan" (Orange pulse ring).

### 3. Exam Question Cards & Option Selectors
- **Question Card Container:** Pure white background, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), 16px corner radius, and 24px inner padding. Includes clear metadata tags (Nomor, Tipe Soal, Bobot).
- **Multiple Choice Radio / Pill Options:** Full-width rounded cards (10px radius) with an explicit letter badge (A, B, C, D) inside a circular border. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` with slate border. Selected: `#EFF6FF` with `#2563EB` 2px border and deep navy label.
- **Complex Question Types:** Benar/Salah and Sesuai/Tidak Sesuai options presented as inline dual-toggle pill switches with instant color-coded affirmative (Emerald) and negative (Rose) feedback upon selection.

### 4. Form Fields & Kisi-Kisi Prompt Input
- **Textareas & Text Inputs:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `#CBD5E1` border, 10px border radius, and generous `14px 16px` padding. Transitions to `#2563EB` border with a 3px soft blue glow on focus.
- **Character & Target Counters:** Right-aligned muted labels (`body-sm`) providing live feedback on question targets (e.g., "10 Stimulus × 3 Soal = 30 Soal").

### 5. Exam Navigation Bar & Timer
- **Sticky Exam Header:** Fixed top toolbar containing exam title, progress indicator bar (percentage completed), and digital timer badge highlighted in soft amber.
- **Question Matrix Grid:** Floating or drawer-based grid displaying question numbers 1 through 30. Unanswered items in slate outline, answered items in solid royal blue, and flagged questions marked with an energetic orange bookmark tag.

### 6. AI Generation Stepper
- Visual progress component with 4 animated stages: *Analisis Kisi-Kisi* → *Menyusun Stimulus* → *Validasi Kunci Jawaban* → *Selesai*. Each active step pulses with primary blue, converting to emerald checkmarks upon completion.
